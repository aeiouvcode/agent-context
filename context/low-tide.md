# LOW TIDE - procedural WebGL2 sea

A single continuously rendered ocean and sky with four scroll-linked light and
weather states. Everything authored in a WebGL2 fragment shader (waves, foam,
specular, sun/moon, clouds, stars, storm flashes); no libraries, accounts, or
backend. Reduced-motion aware.
