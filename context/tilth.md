# TILTH - a small valley life

Original browser farming/life-sim inspired by the craft of Stardew Valley
(never its skin, sprites, names, or content); everything generated in code.
Godot port track active.

## Facts
- Work source: local (Godot project; bundles via relay).
- Backup: `aeiouvcode/tilth-backup` (private), `master`.
- History unsigned; mirrors are byte-identical (real shas preserved).
