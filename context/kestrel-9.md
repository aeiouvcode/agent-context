# KESTREL-9 — on-rails tactical shooter

Sierra 7-class on-rails shooter; the skill core is the shot itself.

## Facts
- Repo: `github.com/aeiouvcode/kestrel-9`; Godot export surface
  `aeiouvcode.github.io/kestrel9-godot`.
- Reference: Sierra 7 (SHD Games). Per Vansh: character moves on their own,
  player strategises, aims and shoots — per-shot bloom, headshot kills,
  hit-stagger. Campaign has a real ending, not SHD's cliffhanger.
- Two builds exist: the JS build and a full GDScript port, both live since
  09-23. Original work, not a port.
